| [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) | Vulnerabilities or reports containing sensitive security details (private process only) |

## Before requesting help

Include:

- Operating-system version
- TokenCue commit (or approximate date of checkout)
- Relevant command or UI path
- Expected result and actual result
- A minimal **redacted** reproduction

Never attach credentials, browser databases, raw provider responses, or unredacted logs.

## What TokenCue cannot do

Provider account, subscription, billing, and service availability questions must be handled by the provider. TokenCue cannot change a provider's quota or restore an external account.
