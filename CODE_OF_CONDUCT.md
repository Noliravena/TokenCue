# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

TokenCue is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and viewpoints
- Focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes
- Protect other people's privacy and never expose credentials or personal data

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private information, sustained disruption, and personal attacks.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, community spaces, and public interactions where someone represents TokenCue.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when behavior is unsafe or harmful.

For routine moderation, contact a maintainer through the private contact options on their GitHub profile. If a report includes a security or privacy risk, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not publish sensitive evidence in a public issue.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where possible, and avoid retaliation against good-faith reporters.
